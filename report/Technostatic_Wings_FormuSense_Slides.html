Slide 2

The Problem We Addressed (PS-1)

A new food product is rarely right the first time, and the gap is closed today by physical trials.

1

A brief arrives

Targets and tolerances, claims, diet and allergens - written down, but not yet a recipe.

2

The first recipe misses something

Texture, pH, water activity, cost or shelf life: rarely all of it at once, and never obviously.

3

The gap is closed by trials

Each one costs material, machine time, analytical work and shelf space.

4

Weeks pass per product

The answer arrives only after the budget has been spent.

5

So PS-1 is not "design a product"

It is: stop paying for trials a model could have avoided.

What we delivered: a working agent that carries a product from a written specification to a validated formulation.

Slide 3

How the Agent Works

1

Understand

The specification becomes targets with tolerances and priorities, read against 95 ingredients.

2

Design

Ingredients fill the category's functional slots and are reconciled to 100 %, acidulants dosed against pH.

3

Predict

Mass balance, energy, water activity, pH, texture, stability and cost - each with a stated uncertainty.

4

Optimise

Coordinate pattern search over the recipe and the process together, with an accepted-move log.

5

Trial and diagnose

A simulated plant with real line faults; residuals in sigma units, a probable cause named with its evidence.

6

Correct and record

Measured offsets become setpoint corrections before the recipe changes; every version is stored.

Database

SQLite is the zero-install default; PostgreSQL is reached by configuration.

Forward-only numbered SQL migrations per dialect, tracked in a schema_migrations table.

One record layer behind the dialect adapter, with the driver imported lazily.

Offline learning

A versioned dataset of 140 rows with a seeded, SHA-256 manifest.

Ridge regression for the objective, calibrated logistic regression for pass/fail.

Split by plant instance, so no plant leaks across folds (80 / 30 / 30).

Operation

Docker: "docker compose up --build" runs against postgres:16.

Schema migrations known to the runner: 1.

Pillow and Pygments stay optional extras; the standard library is the requirement.
